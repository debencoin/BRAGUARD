How to choose a padded sports bra for flag football

Flag football calls for running, reaching, turning and catching. A sports bra should feel secure without restricting those movements. Some athletes also prefer additional cushioning over the chest and outer sides.

1. Start with a comfortable, secure fit

Look for a supportive underband that stays in place when you sprint or raise your arms. Straps should not dig into your shoulders, and the garment should not bunch or ride up. Use the manufacturer's size chart and try on your normal game-day movements.

2. Consider where the padding sits

Padding designs vary. Compare the front and outer-chest coverage, thickness, flexibility and how the pads move with the fabric. BRAGUARD's racerback designs use segmented padding; the 1.0 four-pad style has two front and two outer-side pads.

3. Check mobility and breathability

Test overhead catches, cross-body reaches and twisting. Look for comfortable seams and breathable fabric, especially for hot-weather training and tournaments.

4. Check the rules for your league

Uniform and equipment rules vary across youth, high school, college and adult flag football. Ask your league or event organizer whether padded garments are permitted before game day.

5. Understand what padding can and cannot do

Padded garments may provide cushioning, but no sports bra can guarantee prevention of injury. BRAGUARD has not established injury-prevention performance, and it is not FDA approved. Seek medical advice for persistent pain after a chest impact.
