Women's and girls' flag football gear checklist

Whether you're preparing for your first practice or a weekend tournament, the right gear should help you move freely, stay comfortable and follow your league's rules. Use this checklist as a starting point, then confirm your program's requirements.

Game-day essentials

  • Flag belt and flags: Some leagues provide these; others require a specific approved model.
  • Cleats: Choose footwear appropriate for the field surface. Check league restrictions on metal spikes.
  • Mouthguard: Follow your league's requirements and choose one that fits properly.
  • Uniform or team jersey: Verify number, color and tuck-in rules before arrival.
  • Comfortable base layers: Moisture-wicking tops, shorts or leggings may help with comfort, provided they comply with uniform rules.
  • Supportive sports bra: Look for a secure band, comfortable straps and freedom of movement while catching and sprinting.
  • Water and sun protection: Pack enough water for the session and follow your team's heat-safety plan.

Should you consider a padded sports bra?

Some players prefer a sports bra with segmented cushioning across the front or outer chest. When comparing options, consider pad placement, breathability, fit and whether the garment allows natural catching and throwing motion. Ask your coach or event organizer whether it meets the competition's equipment rules.

What to pack for a tournament

  • Extra socks and a dry change of clothes
  • Refillable water bottle and appropriate snacks
  • Backup hair ties and permitted accessories
  • Team schedule, field directions and emergency contact details
  • Any personal medications or supplies your athlete needs

Before you buy

Equipment rules differ by league, age group and tournament. Confirm permitted protective gear, flag belts and uniform requirements with your organizer. For younger athletes, have a parent or guardian review sizing and equipment choices.
