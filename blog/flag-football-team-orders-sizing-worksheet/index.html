Flag Football Team Orders: A Coach and Parent Sizing Worksheet

Ordering apparel for a girls’ or women’s flag football team is different from choosing one garment for yourself. The useful starting point is an individual fit decision, then an accurate team count. A coach can organize the process; each adult athlete, or a guardian for a younger player, should choose whether the garment and its fit suit her.

Start with a player-led size worksheet

Use a private form or paper sheet with one row per player. Record the size she wants to try, her preferred contact person, and whether she has checked the current size guide. Do not circulate body measurements in a team group chat. For a girls’ team, ask guardians to handle purchasing information and any follow-up.

Field What to record
Player identifier A roster number or private ID
Chosen size The size selected after reviewing the current guide
Fit check Band, straps, reach, sprint and catch movement
Order decision Interested, needs another fit check, or declines
Buyer contact Adult athlete or guardian only

For a try-on, check that the band stays in place, straps remain comfortable, and the player can reach, turn, run and make a routine catch under her intended jersey. Ask what she notices rather than assuming every player wants the same amount of coverage. Check the league’s apparel rules before making a team order.

Build the order count from confirmed choices

  1. Collect size choices privately.
  2. Count only players who have confirmed they want the garment.
  3. Recheck each chosen size and color against the current storefront.
  4. Review the displayed price, shipping, return and availability terms before collecting payment.
  5. Have an adult buyer complete each purchase, or arrange a team purchase only after the seller confirms its terms.

Coach and parent questions to settle before checkout

Does every player need the same size or pad layout?

No. Use each player’s fit and preference to guide the choice. A team worksheet helps with ordering; it should not override individual comfort.

Can a youth player be contacted about an order?

Route sizing, payment and follow-up through a guardian or coach. Do not use individual minors as an outreach list.

Does padding make flag football injury-free?

No such claim is established. BRAGUARD describes added coverage from its pad layout; it does not claim demonstrated impact reduction, injury prevention or cancer prevention.
