What to wear for girls' flag football: a practical game-day guide

For a first practice or a tournament weekend, prioritize clothes that allow easy running, throwing, reaching and catching. Team and league rules always come first, especially for uniforms and protective equipment.

Start with your league's required equipment

Check the team instructions for the jersey, shorts, flag belt, mouthguard and footwear. Some organizations supply belts and jerseys; others ask families to purchase them. Ask about cleat restrictions before choosing footwear.

Choose comfortable athletic layers

Lightweight, moisture-managing athletic clothing can be useful in warm conditions. In colder weather, ask whether fitted layers can be worn under the uniform. Avoid loose items that could interfere with the flag belt or violate the rules.

How to choose a sports bra for flag football

For players who wear sports bras, fit and mobility matter. Look for a band that stays in place and straps that feel comfortable during a sprint, overhead reach and throwing motion. A padded sports bra is an optional apparel choice, not a required piece of equipment for every athlete.

Some players prefer segmented padding over the front and outer chest. BRAGUARD's racerback styles are designed around this layout. Review the actual photos and sizes before deciding whether a style suits your needs.

What to pack for the sideline

  • Water bottle and weather-appropriate sun protection
  • Spare socks and a dry change of clothes
  • Hair ties and any league-permitted accessories
  • Team schedule, field location and emergency contacts

Before kickoff

Try the full outfit together, including the flag belt. Check that nothing restricts arm movement and that the uniform meets the event rules. For younger players, parents or guardians should assist with sizing and purchases.
